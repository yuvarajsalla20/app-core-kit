# Rules for this kit

- Every word the kit shows (captions, taglines, sub-lines, placeholders, notes, messages, buttons) must be professional and consistent. Follow the "Writing rule" in app-settings-kit's README: Title Case for titles, rows and buttons; calm, complete sentences for captions and messages; "e.g.," for examples; no chatty fragments, slang or exclamation marks.
- Apps never override kit words or layout: change the kit default instead (a major version when the change is visible).
